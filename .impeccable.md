## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters and hiring managers in tech and data engineering. They are looking for technical excellence, analytical rigor, and creative problem-solving.

### Brand Personality
Creative, Visionary, Analytical. The voice is technical and precise, but the tone is evocative and high-impact, suggesting a "cosmic" scale of thinking.

### Aesthetic Direction
Deep Midnight Blue and Cyan cosmic theme. Uses bento grids, glassmorphism, and atmospheric background animations (stars, comets) to create a "digital space" feel.

### Design Principles
1. **Precision & Rigor**: Maintain clean lines, consistent spacing, and technical typography (monospaced accents).
2. **Cosmic Perspective**: Use depth, translucency, and motion to create an expansive, high-impact feel.
3. **Adaptive Clarity**: Ensure the sophisticated desktop layout (bento grid, horizontal scrolls) adapts intelligently to mobile without losing its character.
4. **Refined Spacing**: Avoid crowding; let elements breathe to emphasize their importance and the "void" of space.
