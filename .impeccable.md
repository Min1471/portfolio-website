## Design Context

### Users
Tech Recruiters looking for a blend of technical expertise (Electrical Engineering + Master of IT) and a creative, approachable personality. The interface should make it easy to verify skills and achievements while leaving a memorable impression.

### Brand Personality
Creative, approachable, yet professional. It should feel like a bridge between the precision of engineering and the accessibility of modern web design.

### Aesthetic Direction
A "Minimalist Night Sky" theme. This involves deep dark blues (not just flat black), subtle star-like textures, and elegant silver/cyan accents. It avoids the "neon/gamer" trope in favor of a more sophisticated, atmospheric feel.

### Design Principles
- **Atmospheric Depth**: Use layers of subtle gradients and varying star sizes to create a sense of space.
- **Elegance over Intensity**: Prefer silver and soft cyan accents over harsh glows.
- **Clarity & Focus**: Content remains the hero; the night sky background should provide context, not distraction.
- **Smooth Continuity**: Transitions between sections should feel like moving through space—fluid and calm.
