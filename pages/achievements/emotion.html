Deep Learning & Sentiment Analysis

A comparative analysis of classical ML and advanced Deep Learning models for multi-class emotion detection in text.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

This research project focused on evaluating the performance of various machine learning architectures in classifying human emotions from textual data. By benchmarking classical models against state-of-the-art transformer-based approaches, the study provides critical insights into the strengths and limitations of different NLP methodologies.

MODELS & ANALYSIS

Architectures

Compared Logistic Regression, SVM, and Random Forest against GRU, LSTM, and Bi-LSTM networks.

Feature Extraction

Implemented TF-IDF and word embeddings to capture semantic nuances in emotional language.

Performance

Conducted rigorous hyperparameter tuning and benchmarking using BERT as the state-of-the-art baseline.
