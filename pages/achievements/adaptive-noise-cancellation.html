Overview

Ambient noise is a constant problem for online learning and remote work. I designed and trained a fully connected network to suppress environmental noise in real time, studying how network design, including leaky ReLU activation, affects speech enhancement.

Results

Real-time performance

Improved PESQ by 10% while keeping latency below 300 ms.

Evaluation

Tested on Thai and English speech, using PESQ and STOI to measure quality and intelligibility.

Publication

Wrote the conference paper and presented the findings at IEEE InCITT 2022 to academic and industry audiences.
