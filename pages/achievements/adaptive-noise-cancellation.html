RESEARCH OVERVIEW

This project addresses the challenges of ambient noise in online learning and remote work environments. By employing a deep learning approach to signal processing, we developed a system capable of real-time audio mitigation. The research focuses on the efficacy of fully connected networks and leaky ReLU activation in speech enhancement.
