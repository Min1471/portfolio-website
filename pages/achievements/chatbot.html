Presentation roast bot

Terrible Idea Hackathon, 2023

A voice-driven AI chatbot that listens to a presentation and roasts it back.

Back to hackathons

Overview

Built for a hackathon that rewards gloriously bad ideas. The bot listens to a live presentation, works out what is being said, and responds with a consistent, humorous roast, which meant getting speech input, language understanding and personality all working together in real time.

How it works

Listening

Voice input from the presentation, processed with minimal delay so the roast lands while it is still relevant.

Understanding

Transformer models pick out what the presenter is actually saying.

Personality

Prompting tuned for a consistent, humorous tone.
